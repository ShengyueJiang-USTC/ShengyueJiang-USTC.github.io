I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Science and Technology of China.

My research interests include **complex networks**, **graph learning**, **social bot detection**, and **trustworthy artificial intelligence**.
I focus on understanding organized behaviors, structural patterns, and information manipulation in online social networks.

My current work explores multi-view graph representation learning, fuzzy community modeling.

I am also interested in developing open-source research tools and reproducible methods for network analysis and social media security.

## Research Interests

- Complex Networks and Network Science
- Graph Neural Networks and Graph Representation Learning
- Social Bot Detection
- Community Detection and Fuzzy Modeling
- AI Security and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence
- Large Language Models for Graph Reasoning
